# Data Availability

The original PIRATA observations are official external source data and are referenced for this study; the raw PIRATA archive is not redistributed in the project release. WOA23 is likewise an official external source and its raw archive is not redistributed.

Author-generated derived records used for verification and reproducibility are available in the frozen archive at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23120236. The archive scope and reuse conditions are documented in `DATA_LICENSE_RECOMMENDATION.md` and `DATA_REDISTRIBUTION_POLICY.md`.
